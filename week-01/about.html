About Me

I'm Raza - Ullah Sajid, a Computer Science student and Web Developer from Kot Sabzal, Pakistan. I enjoy turning practical requirements into digital products, taking work from early structure and interface decisions through implementation, testing and launch. My web work spans responsive websites and applications built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, TypeScript and Next.js, with a focus on clear, maintainable code.

I'm also growing my mobile development experience: I have built and published a native iOS app with Swift, and a second app is in progress. Experience

Professional Approach

I work as a solo developer, taking responsibility for the connected parts of a project instead of treating design, implementation and delivery as separate handoffs. This gives clients a direct point of contact and keeps decisions grounded in the project's goals, users and technical requirements.

End-to-End Ownership

I stay involved from understanding the requirements through building and preparing the final product. That continuity helps keep the original goals visible as implementation details take shape.

Thoughtful Implementation

I aim for responsive interfaces, reusable structures and practical technical choices. I consider how pages and features fit together, not just how an individual screen looks.

Learning Through Delivery

I build on completed work while continuing to develop my skills. In mobile development, I have published a Swift iOS app and continue learning with guidance from a senior iOS developer.
