Services

From shaping an early idea to delivering a polished, launch-ready result, I help turn practical goals into digital work that is clear, reliable and built for its audience. My services cover web and app development, interface implementation and creative production. Every project is scoped around its needs, with thoughtful communication, careful execution and support through review and delivery.

Web Development

I build responsive websites and web applications with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React and TypeScript. The focus is on clear page structure, maintainable components and a consistent experience across screen sizes, with room to evolve as your needs grow.

Front-End Development

I turn product requirements and visual direction into interactive front ends using reusable React and TypeScript components. From navigation and page states to considered motion and responsive behavior, I aim for interfaces that feel intuitive and remain straightforward to maintain.

UI Implementation

I translate supplied designs or references into accurate, mobile-first interfaces using tools such as Tailwind CSS and CSS variables. Layout, spacing, typography and reusable styling are handled consistently so the finished pages work well on both small and large screens.

iOS App Development

I build native iOS apps with Swift, with experience taking an app through development and publication on the App Store. I focus on clear, purposeful screens, reliable interactions and a smooth experience across the app's core flows. We can discuss your idea, key features and release goals to shape a practical project scope.

Client Product Development

I work directly from client goals to plan and deliver practical digital products, coordinating the key pieces from interface implementation through launch. Where relevant, this includes on-page SEO fundamentals such as page titles, metadata and clear content structure.

Graphic Design

I create graphic content for digital platforms, social media and brand communication. Each piece is shaped around its intended audience and use, with attention to visual hierarchy, consistency and clear messaging.

Video Editing

I edit video for creator and client projects, including YouTube-focused content. Editing can include arranging footage, tightening pacing and refining the presentation so the final video supports its intended message and format.

Photo Editing

I refine images for digital content and design projects, with adjustments guided by the intended look and where the image will be used. The goal is a clean, consistent visual that fits naturally with the wider project.

How I Work

A clear, collaborative process helps keep the work focused, makes decisions easier and gives you a chance to review progress before delivery.

01

Understand the Goal

We discuss your audience, objectives, references and essential requirements. This gives us a shared understanding of what the project needs to achieve.

02

Define the Scope

I outline the deliverables, relevant platform and priorities before work begins. Clear scope helps set practical expectations around features, content and timing.

03

Create & Refine

I build or create the agreed work, then review it with you. Your feedback helps identify adjustments and keep the result aligned with the original goals.

04

Review & Deliver

Before handoff, I check the finished work and address agreed refinements. For web projects, this includes preparing the final pages for deployment and launch.
